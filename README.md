# 🍽️ Recipe Meal App - mood

Recipe Meal App built with Flutter - A smart food app that suggests what to eat based on your **mood**.

Instead of searching for recipes, the app asks you how you feel, how hungry you are, and what you crave, then recommends the perfect meal for you.

## ✨ Features

- **Mood-Based Recommendations:** Choose your mood and get meals that fit it
- **Onboarding Flow:** Beautiful and smooth intro screens in Arabic
- **How Hungry Are You?** - Adjust recommendations based on your appetite level
- **Meal Preferences:** Filter by type, ingredients, and what you want to avoid
- **Recipe Details:** Full ingredients, steps, calories and cooking time
- **Clean UI:** Modern Arabic UI with red / white theme, built for mobile
- **State Management:** Clean architecture with Flutter

## 📱 App Flow

1. Welcome Screen - مزاج
2. What's your mood today?
3. How hungry are you?
4. What are you craving?
5. Get your personalized meal suggestion
6. View recipe details & start cooking
